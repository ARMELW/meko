# Subscription management specification

- Choose and subscribe to a plan (monthly or yearly).
- Two types of subscriptions are available: Individual or Family.
- Make a secure payment.
- Manage payment methods: Add or modify a payment method.
- View payment history.


# Flexible subscription management specification

- Change subscription plan.
- Enable or disable automatic renewal.
- Access payment history.
- Receive automatic email notifications for renewals or failed payments.
